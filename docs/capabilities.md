# CS-SEM capabilities and evidence

This table separates available workflows from features that have been tested
against stored simulation truth. `implemented` means a public path exists and
has software tests; it does not imply broad methodological validation.
`validated` means checked evidence exists for the named scenarios and metrics
below, not that every design or real dataset is covered. `experimental` marks
outputs that are available for exploration but do not have calibrated
confirmatory inference.

| Capability | Status | Evidence | Conditions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinal, continuous, mixed-scale, and manifest measurement declarations | implemented | [`specify_measurement()` and `mixed_items()`](../R/model.R), [measurement tests](../tests/testthat/test-fit.R) | Each construct is one-dimensional; each indicator belongs to one construct. Mixed/continuous declarations are implemented, but the released supported envelope below is narrower. |
| Ordinal measurement recovery inside the recorded release envelope | validated | [`supported_envelope.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/supported_envelope.csv), [`measurement_confirmation.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/measurement_confirmation.csv), [`release_gates.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/release_gates.csv) | Evidence covers one-dimensional ordinal manifestation blocks with at least four indicators, `n >= 200`, loading at least `.70`, and at most 10% item missingness. The artifact reports 70 measurement jobs, 20 inside the envelope, 100% convergence inside it, and passing release gates. Cross-loadings, overlap, sparse categories, and local dependence are exploratory conditions. |
| Partial, listwise, and error-on-missing policies with sample accounting | implemented | [`fit_states()`](../R/fit.R), [`sample_accounting()`](../R/missing-data.R), [missing-data tests](../tests/testthat/test-missing-data.R) | Partial item responses contribute their observed likelihood terms; this is not general-purpose imputation. Structural complete-case policies remain separate from measurement missingness. |
| Cross-fitted scores, reusable measurement splits, and outer held-out evaluation | implemented | [`make_splits()`](../R/splits.R), [`validate_outer()`](../R/outer-validation.R), [split tests](../tests/testthat/test-splits.R), [outer-validation tests](../tests/testthat/test-outer-validation.R) | Outer-test metrics describe held-out records; internal selector metrics are separate and must not be reported as held-out results. Grouped/time partitions have their own support constraints. |
| Linear, monotone, and low-complexity smooth structural selection | validated | [`structural_confirmation.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/structural_confirmation.csv), [`release_gates.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/release_gates.csv), [selector description](associational-structure.md) | Confirmation artifacts cover the named linear, monotone, smooth, null, omitted-predictor, and downstream scenarios. The selector considers declared edges, retains at most one nonlinear edge per outcome, and does not discover arbitrary interactions or feedback. |
| New-record structural prediction and marginal contrasts | implemented | [`predict()`](../R/prediction.R), [`marginal_contrast()`](../R/categorical.R), [prediction tests](../tests/testthat/test-prediction.R) | Predictions use the fitted measurement encoders and locked-score basis. Contrast intervals condition on the selected shapes unless repeated selection is explicitly requested where supported. Predictions are associational. |
| Bootstrap and errors-in-variables correction | implemented | [`bootstrap_model()`](../R/bootstrap.R), [`associate()`](../R/structure.R), [bootstrap tests](../tests/testthat/test-bootstrap.R) | Correction is available for supported linear/monotone score models when reliability is available; smooth-edge correction is unavailable. Fixed-selection intervals condition on the selected shapes. This table does not assert calibrated coverage for every estimator/design combination. |
| Posterior uncertainty draws and information respondent weighting | experimental | [`fit_states()`](../R/fit.R), [`associate()`](../R/structure.R), [fit tests](../tests/testthat/test-fit.R), [structural tests](../tests/testthat/test-structure.R) | Draws are not calibrated confidence intervals and are excluded from the release-validation evidence. Information weighting is off by default and is not a survey-weight estimator. |
| Group comparisons, common-anchor measurement diagnostics, defined contrasts, and constrained linear paths | implemented | [`group_comparison()` and `measurement_invariance()`](../R/groups.R), [`contrast()` and constraints](../R/contrasts.R), [group tests](../tests/testthat/test-groups.R), [contrast tests](../tests/testthat/test-contrasts.R) | Group item diagnostics are conditional on a pooled common-anchor score; they are not equality-constrained configural, metric, or scalar invariance tests. Constraints apply to supported linear locked-score estimates and do not create a covariance SEM. |
| Causal effect and mediation estimands | implemented | [`causal_effect()`](../R/causal.R), [`causal_indirect_effect()`](../R/causal-mediation.R), [causal tests](../tests/testthat/test-causal.R), [mediation tests](../tests/testthat/test-causal-mediation.R) | Calls require explicit estimands, adjustment choices, and temporal-order declarations where required. The software cannot verify no unmeasured confounding, consistency, positivity, or the substantive correctness of those assumptions. A causal label is not proof that assumptions hold. |
| Registered mediation recovery scenarios | validated | [`mediation_validation.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/mediation_validation.csv), [`mediation_validation_summary.csv`](../tests/internal/validation_results/mediation_validation_summary.csv) | Stored simulations use named single, parallel, and serial data-generating scenarios with independent truth. Replication counts and design assumptions are recorded in the artifacts; they do not establish performance under other mediator structures or study conditions. |
| Explicit estimator-scope boundaries | implemented | [method specification](method-spec.md), [SEM gap audit](gaps.md) | CS-SEM is not a global covariance-structure SEM. Covariance-only input, chi-square/CFI/TLI/RMSEA/SRMR fit indices, formative and higher-order constructs, cross-loadings, correlated item errors, and simultaneous feedback models are outside the current estimator. |

`supported_envelope()` returns the thresholds and evidence counts from the
installed release artifact. Treat it as a report about registered scenarios
and metrics, not a universal sample-size formula, a power analysis, or a
guarantee for a new study.

For the worked analysis, see the [workflow vignette](../vignettes/cssem-workflow.Rmd).
For the scope and transformations, see the [method specification](method-spec.md)
and [associational-structure guide](associational-structure.md).
